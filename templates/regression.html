2. Feature Selection & Leakage Prevention

Included Features

  • Crop — Primary determinant of biological yield potential (e.g. cereals vs plantation).
  • Season — Captures photoperiod and weather differences (Kharif, Rabi, Summer, Winter).
  • State — Geospatial proxy for regional soil quality and agronomic practices.
  • Area — Scale of cultivation (hectares).
  • Annual_Rainfall — Moisture availability throughout the crop calendar.
  • Fertilizer & Pesticide — Chemical nutrient and crop protection inputs.

Excluded Features (Leakage Avoidance)

  • Production — Yield = Production / Area. Including Production would leak the target directly and prevent pre-harvest forecasting.
  • Crop_Median_Yield — Derived directly from historical target distributions.
  • UNDERPERFORMANCE_FLAG — Operational anomaly label computed from yield.
