About target leakage prevention & the underperformance alert

In agricultural forecasting, Production is the product of Area × Yield. Including Production as an input feature would create direct target leakage and render a model useless prior to harvest. CropSense therefore strictly isolates Production, predicting Yield solely from environmental conditions (rainfall), input dosages (fertilizer & pesticide), land scale, season, and geography.

Furthermore, CropSense creates an operational Underperformance Alert Flag when seasonal rainfall (>800 mm) and fertilization are ample, yet realized harvest yield falls into the lowest quartile for that specific crop, providing early warning for pest outbreaks or localized crop stress.
