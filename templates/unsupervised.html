2. Dimensionality Reduction — PCA & t-SNE Manifold

Principal Component Analysis (PCA)

High-dimensional agricultural spaces are projected onto orthogonal axes of maximum variance via covariance matrix eigen-decomposition:

  • PC1 (First Principal Component): Explains {{ pc1_var }} of variance, primarily capturing overall agricultural input intensity (fertilizer & pesticide usage).
  • PC2 (Second Principal Component): Explains {{ pc2_var }} of variance, separating rainfed low-yield crops from high-biomass plantation systems.
  • Cumulative Variance: PC1 + PC2 capture {{ cum_var }} of total dataset information.

t-SNE Non-Linear Manifold

t-Distributed Stochastic Neighbor Embedding minimizes Kullback-Leibler divergence between joint probabilities in high-dimensional and low-dimensional space, preserving localized neighborhood structures among crop clusters.

5. Density-Based Clustering — DBSCAN & Noise Isolation

DBSCAN Mechanics (ε = 1.1, MinPts = 6)

  • Core Samples: Points residing in high-density multi-dimensional regions having ≥ 6 neighbors within radius ε.
  • Border Samples: Points reachable from core samples but having lower density.
  • Noise Points (Outliers): Isolated observations located in sparse feature regions flagged as potential anomalies ({{ dbscan_noise_pct }}% of data).

6. Unsupervised Anomaly Detection

Anomaly detection is performed without ground-truth labels by computing the PCA reconstruction error for each observation: $$ ext{Score}(x) = \|x - \hat{x}_{ ext{PCA}}\|^2$$ Observations that deviate sharply from normal agricultural correlations (e.g. catastrophic harvest collapse during favorable rainfall) yield high reconstruction error and are isolated as multi-dimensional anomalies.
